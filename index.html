About MANGO Hair Studio

Our hair salon offers a comprehensive range of hair services tailored to meet the unique needs of each client. Our Cancellation Policy

24-Hour Notice · 50% Fee

We ask that you please reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours before the beginning of your appointment, or you may be charged a cancellation fee of 50% of the price of your scheduled appointment.

Out of respect for the time that our stylists invest in reserving your appointment, fees will be charged for any appointments canceled or rescheduled with less than 24 hours' notice. The same fees will also apply to all no-call, no-shows.

The fee must be paid before booking another appointment. The fees are as follows: 50% of any combo or single service.

Without advance notice of cancellations, our stylists are unable to offer accurate availability to you and others. Late arrivals of 15 minutes or more may be subject to having one or more of their services canceled or rescheduled.

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please call the salon and our receptionists will gladly answer them. If you couldn't reach us by phone at (407) 730-4347, you can also send us an email at info@mangobeauty.us. We are not accepting voice messages or text messages on phone number (407) 730-4347.
